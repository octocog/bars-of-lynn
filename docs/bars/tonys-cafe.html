Night of 17 August 1986

14 Tony's Cafe

Come on in, this place is on the street below "Rocky's Gym". An older crowd graces this avid, older bar with painted tin ceilings (brown).

The ceilings were high and so was the elder clientele as one fine gentleman sauntered over to the juke box to make such awesome rock & roll selections as "Paper Roses" - Marie Osmond. Some slow dancing was present but sitting was king.

The men's room entrance was unique and quite challenging, it narrowed as you neared your destiny - no fat people made it into this restroom - I guess they opted for the women's room.

The classic, vintage Clydesdales were present. But let's not forget that Tony's offered fine cuisine: $1.00 sandwiches, your choice: Tuna or Egg Salad!

Photograph: a dark night exposure of a corner building, a streetlight flaring at upper right, lit signs in the ground floor windows.
