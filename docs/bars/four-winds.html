Night of 10 September 1986

39 The Four Winds

Located on scenic Sluice Pond, this bar is similar in overall ambiance to Buchanan's. More of a shack than a building. The evening we were there, a cool breeze was wafting over the water and through the windows to cool the dart players during their exertions. Bathroom is comparable in size to Buchanan's also and with the sink (!!!) up around your chin, washing your hands is tricky but throwing up is cake. A good pool table, too.

Photograph: a small single storey building with a pitched roof and stone or shingle walls, set among trees.
