Night of 8 September 1986

35 The Jib

The bar is long, narrow, and dark with a limited bar at the front. It was a small crowd the night we were there - though they all seemed to be enjoying themselves. Our own senses were starting to dull and cloud at this point. This is another basic color T.V., video game, and miniature bowling establishment. There are no darts. We moved on.

Photograph: a low flat roofed building with a shingled awning. A large sign on the roof reads "JIB / RESTAURANT". A car parked alongside.
