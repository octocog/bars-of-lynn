Night of 14 August 1986

09 Max's Pub

A tight bar, with a warm, but professional air. The kitchen is easily viewed from a bar stool - the food looks good.

We were impressed by the frosted mugs and the reasonable prices of draft beer available. This was a classy place - they had their own matches!

The draft Moosehead was cool and crisp - duly impressed. You can't go wrong here.

Photograph: a street corner at night, a large billboard reading "Enjoy the Best... Ice Cream", a streetlight, and a tall brick building beyond.

Note
