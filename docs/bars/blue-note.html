Night of 12 August 1986

06 The Blue Note

A very nice, open, barnlike atmosphere with plenty of dart boards. A large capacity establishment with Olive Oyl on the Ladies Room, Popeye on the Men's. Bumper Pool you fancy? - Here's the place and a large screen T.V. to boot.

A real happening place, complete with food. And we had the fortune of observing a possibly underaged young lady stagger over to greet Olive Oyl, push through the door, do the obvious, then stumble back our only to be escorted from the bar.

A real OK place.

Photograph: a long low windowless wall, very dark exposure, with two small signs.
