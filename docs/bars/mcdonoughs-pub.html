Night of 10 September 1986

36 McDonough's Pub

An Irish bar without Draft Guiness - a cruel, cruel hoax. That being said right off - let's move on. The panelling has obviously been put up be someone with a whole lot of remodelled basements under his belt. The carpetted ceiling posts are a touch any drunk would appreciate. An awful lot of furniture to catch the errant foot however, so don't get careless. Looks like some food beyond the standard bar fare might be available here so you might ask for a menu. Darts could be played but you'd have to get permission to move some tables.

Looks like a big place to go after Beano.

Photograph: a brick building shot along its flank. A projecting sign in script reads "McDonough" above large block letters "PUB" and a shamrock.
