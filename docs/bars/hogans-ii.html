Night of 8 September 1986

32 Hogan's II

A brightly lit bar, scenically located across from Bennie's Pizza, Hogan's II sported a young crowd. The bar (horseshoe-type) was well stocked and you couldn't find an open stool. Even most of the tables were occupied.

You'll never be bored here either, not with the Harlem Globetrotters pinball, pooltable and two cole T.V.'s, no sir.

A great place to come and watch "the ball game".

Photograph: a painted fascia sign reading "HOGAN'S II" in ornate lettering above a window and a panelled door.
