Night of 8 September 1986

33 The 595 Cafe

More mounted fish - tuna and sailfish - this time. There's no TV and the people at the bar speak Polish, so bring your dictionary. The bar is well lit and well managed. While these reviewers weren't interested in food, it looks like it might be a good place. The crowd here looks like it would also be at home in the Shawmut Grille so age might be a problem. Probably a good cheap date. No darts.

Photograph: a projecting sign reading "595 CAFE" with a bow tie device below it and a smaller "AIR CONDITIONED" panel beneath. Clapboard buildings along a narrow street.

Reading notes
