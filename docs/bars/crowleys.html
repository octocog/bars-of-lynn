Night of 23 August 1986

21 Crowley's

Crowley's had a quiet but busy Saturday night atmosphere, no seating at the bar. We surmised that Crowley's was a good place to be on a Saturday night in Lynn because we recognized our bartender from Wortman's (kinda like seeing where the truckers' stop to find a good place to eat).

Oddly enough, there were two T.V.'s at opposite ends of the standard 30 foot, one sided bar with the Bears vs. the Cards on one set, Golden Girls on the other.

Obviously, the management was aiming at drawing a varied cross-section.

Photograph: a clapboard and brick block. A projecting sign reads "Crowley's PUB RESTAURANT" with a second sign below reading "Restaurant". A LIQUOR STORE sign on the brick building beyond. Cars parked along the street.
