Night of 25 August 1986

26 Ziggy's

No name outside so you gotta know where you're going and where you're goins it's mounted fish and a lot of them. Ziggy must know a good taxidermist. In fact, he should introduce him to Mr. Cronin (Cronin's Cafe #11) who has a lot of snapshots up on the wall from his cronies (get it?) and a lot of dead unstuffed fish; it's hard to tell whose eyes are more glazed (the fish or the fisherman). This bar has darts and it's pretty low-key despite the bank vault front door. Bring a friend.

Two photographs on this page. Upper: a very dark exposure of a building with pickup trucks parked in front. Lower: a two storey building, stone or stucco at ground level with brick above, a central doorway.
