Night of 7 August 1986

04 39 Buffum Street

Now that's practicality for you: Why not name your bar by its address - that way you can always find it!!

OK, OK, this place has a reputation, well earned I'm sure. But it's a lively place and if you like darts and a lot of AC DC with Angus Young on the VIDEO JUKEBOX - this is your place.

It may even be the place for lunch - they've got specials. The softball team was present when we hit the 39 - but they welcomed us and even played darts with us - one guy let us borrow his darts for a good 2 hours - what a pal. Sometimes reputations are earned, sometimes it's just rotten luck. My vote is for the latter when talking about the 39.

Photograph: brick building with four striped awnings on the upper floor and a large painted sign reading "39 BUFFUM STREET" above the ground floor.
