Night of 19 August 1986

20 The Klub Kar

Dynamically located 'neath the railroad tracks in Lynn, this establishment is graced with a long, long bar, a cigarette machine named "Donna" (maybe she should meet B.B. King's guitar), and a top hat, gloves and cane on the men's room door. It had a happy atmosphere - evident from the abundant clown paraphernalia: clown paintings, clown puppets, clown plaques, just about every type of clown memorabilia one could think of. Also has live music on occasion.

There was a good crowd for a Tuesday. Always a safe getaway - the Klub Kar has three exits.

Photograph: a brick building directly beneath an elevated railway structure, a Bud Light sign over the door and "KLUB KAR" painted large on the wall. A billboard for Carlton cigarettes is visible above the tracks.

Reading notes

  • Location clue: "'neath the railroad tracks in Lynn". Strong, physical, and visible in the photograph. The elevated line through Lynn narrows this considerably.
