Night of 12 August 1986

07 Smuggler's Cove

A nice angular place with good music to greet you (CCR, Doors, George). Kinda a family atmosphere, almost sophisticated (for Lynn). Comando was on the screen, a place with HBO, WOW what a class "A" joint - these guys don't fool around.

The men's room is small and overworked. The bar was very crowded, no room at the bar - but we found room at the tables. Sorta hated to leave - but when you've got a job to do..........

Photograph: very dark exposure, a lit sign reading "S PUB" or similar at the centre of a large dark facade.
