Night of 19 August 1986

17 The Brandy Barrel

The Barrel was on low key when we hit it - almost all the lights were off. There were just enough on for the barmaids to serve us two Buds.

The atmosphere seemed to be good - a potential was here but we couldn't quite get the outlook on the entire place.

The barmaids were friendly and the beer was cheap.

We were on a mission from God, so we moved on. Nothing special here.
