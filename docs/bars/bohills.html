Night of 2 August 1986

02 Bohill's

★ Gold Star

For those of you who like draft Guiness , authentic live Irish music and good food - this is the place in Lynn for you. Clean, nicely laid out bar with pleasant decor and of course, an all Irish staff. A good place to take the family, almost yuppie- but it's not crowded enough and the prices are too reasonable. Deserves the Gold Star even at this early stage.

You could bring mom here. It's a good show - never stuffy.

Photograph: brick building, hanging sign in blackletter reading "Bohill's Irish Pub" above a recessed storefront with pilasters.
