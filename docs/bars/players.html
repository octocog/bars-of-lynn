Night of 23 August 1986

23 Player's

The two traveller's entered the bright pseudo sport's bar from the windy night to find two lucky seats right at the corner of the bar.

But as they nervously watched a heavily unbalanced ceiling fan overhead, they wondered about the fate of the last patrons that had sat on those stools.

One coupled danced (looked like a mother/son-in-law combo).

Player's seems to proclaim itself a sports bar but we found this proclamation questionable - there were two T.V.s in the place: one had a Phil Collins concert on, the other the News.

Nice clean bathrooms with portholes on the doors - nice touch.

Photograph: a brick building with an arched doorway, shot from close up along the pavement. A projecting sign reads "PLAYERS / RESTAURANT & SPORTS BAR".
