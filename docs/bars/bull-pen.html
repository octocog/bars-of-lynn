Night of 14 August 1986

12 Bull Pen Restaurant

The Bull Pen is an airy, open bar with a light-hearted atmosphere.

We noted the two wild boars mounted on the wall behind the bar and were promptly briefed on the dental characteristics and the killing habits of boars by the two barmaids. They seemed quite knowledgeable.

The Bull Pen had their own Softball and Dart Team with a couple nice dart boards.

A neat bar with a friendly staff made the Bull Pen a nice round-off to the evening.

Photograph: a brick facade with an arched doorway, two small framed signs either side of the door, and neighbouring shopfronts to the right.
