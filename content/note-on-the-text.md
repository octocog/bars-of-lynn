## Full version

### A note on the text

Everything here is reproduced as it was typed in 1986. Spelling, punctuation and
typing errors are left alone. So is the language, some of which belongs to its moment
and to two men writing for an audience of each other, and would not be written that
way now.

Nothing has been corrected, softened or cut. An archive that quietly edits its own
source is worth less than no archive at all, and a reader in another forty years
should be able to see what was actually on the page.

The photographs are reproduced from the same Xeroxed copy, at whatever quality the
Xerox left them.

## Notes on the choices

**It does not apologise.** The document is a primary source and the site's job is to
carry it, not to referee it. A long preamble would make the reader brace for something
far worse than what is actually there.

**It does not quote the offending passages.** Naming them in advance turns the note
into a list, and sends readers hunting. A general statement does the work.

**It gives the reason, once.** "An archive that edits its source is not one" is the
whole argument, and it is the same principle the Paul Donato archive runs on.

**Placement.** Best on the About page and linked from the foot of every bar page,
rather than printed at the top of each entry. It is context, not a warning label, and
repeating it on all forty pages would make it louder than the document.
