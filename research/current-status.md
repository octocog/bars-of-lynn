# What became of the 40 bars — research pass 1

Researched 2026-10-01 by web search only. **No library or microfilm access.**
Confidence levels are the researcher's own. Nothing here should go on the site as
fact without the stated source being checked, and the LOW items should not go on
the site at all yet.

---

## Still trading today

**THE FOUR WINDS** — 265 Broadway, Lynn, on Sluice Pond. **HIGH confidence.**
Established 1939. Described by the Boston Globe in August 2023 as "the oldest
continuously operated pub in the city of Lynn", which necessarily spans 1986.
This is the strongest survivor on the list.
Source: Boston Globe, 22 Aug 2023, "At Four Winds in Lynn, pub grub with a water view".

**HOGAN'S II** — 160 Franklin St, Lynn. **HIGH that it trades today; MEDIUM on
unbroken continuity.** Same name, stable address, currently listed as active.
No document found bridging 1986 to the present.

**BUCHANAN'S** — possibly today's Buchanan Cafe, 648 Eastern Ave. **LOW.** A customer
review describes it as open "over 50 years", consistent with pre 1986 roots, but no
documentary history found. NOTE: this appears in the researcher's summary but is
missing from their own findings table, so treat as unverified until re-checked.

**THE 50 CLUB** — now Brickyard 50 Club / DiFilipo's, Blossom St. **HIGH.**
Founded about 1963 by the DeFilipo family, relocated within the Brickyard during
1978 urban renewal, still family owned, 60th anniversary covered in 2023.
**This is one of the two bars Murnane and Albert explicitly skipped.**
Sources: Lynn Journal and Itemlive, both September 2023.

## The best documented ending

**SMUGGLER'S COVE** — Washington Street. Foreclosed, sat vacant for years, and the
historic flatiron building was **demolished in 2018** and replaced with condominiums
under the Washington Street Gateway District redevelopment. **HIGH on the demolition,
MEDIUM that it is the same bar visited in 1986.**
Source: Itemlive, 31 Jul 2018, "Condos are on tap at the former Smuggler's Cove Pub site in Lynn".

---

## The best successor story

**THE GOLDEN CIRCLE** — closed by about 2004, when Rolly and Wendy Hayes acquired
"the old Golden Circle liquor license" and opened **Rolly's Golden Circle** in the
former Equitable Bank building in Wyoma Square. Later renamed **Rolly's Tavern on
the Square**, 338 Broadway, still trading. A genuine licence lineage, though not the
same building. **MEDIUM to HIGH.**
Source: EDIC Lynn, 2012.

### Explicitly ruled out, do not conflate
- **THE BULL PEN**: a Bull Pen Sports Bar exists in the area but dates to about 2010. Name coincidence.
- **PLAYER'S**: Players Sports Bar, 858 Western Ave, about 2008. Name coincidence.
- **THE CRYSTAL GRILL**: the famous Crystal Grill is in Greenwood, Mississippi. Unrelated.
- **THE CORINTHIAN CLUB**: not the Corinthian Yacht Club, Marblehead.
- **THE KLUB KAR**: not the Vero Beach, Florida taxi service.
- **THE JIB**: not "J-Bar".
- **TONY'S CAFE / TONY'S**: today's Tony's Pub & Grill, 196 Franklin St, claims a 1984 start, but the 1986 document lists Tony's Cafe (#14) and Tony's (#31) as two separate bars and nothing distinguishes which, if either, is the survivor.

## The gap

**Roughly two thirds of the forty returned no Lynn specific source at all:**
Wortmanns, Bohill's, Mister Max's, Corcoran's, Cronin's, Conti's, Mel's Stateroom,
Scotch Mist, Corinthian Club, Klub Kar, Crowley's, Ziggy's, The Arena, The Hall of
Fame, Burke's 757, Ruthie's, The Bus Stop, The Jib, McDonough's, Roadhouse,
Market Square Pub, Union Station, The Venice.

This is a real gap, not a failure of effort. Closing it needs:
- Lynn Public Library local archives and newspaper microfilm
- Massachusetts corporate dissolution filings searched by name
- A Lynn nostalgia Facebook group, where this kind of memory actually lives

**Most productive source by far was Itemlive**, the Daily Item, but only where a
property or redevelopment angle gave the paper a reason to write.
